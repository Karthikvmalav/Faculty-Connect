# Faculty Connect: Human-Centered Design for Faculty Engagement

Faculty Connect is a Design Thinking project focused on improving collaboration and community among faculty members at IIIT Hyderabad. Through user research and iterative design, we identified key challenges affecting faculty interaction and proposed a sustainable engagement ecosystem to foster meaningful connections beyond formal work.

## Problem Statement

**How might we create opportunities for faculty to connect personally beyond work interactions so that they can enhance their sense of inclusion within the faculty community?**

## Approach

The project followed the Design Thinking process:

- Conducted 16 faculty interviews and analyzed 11 survey responses
- Performed affinity mapping, empathy mapping, persona creation, and user journey mapping
- Generated and prioritized 20+ How-Might-We statements using the RICE and Impact–Alignment frameworks
- Validated concepts through faculty feedback and iterative refinement

## Solution

The final proposal is a Faculty Connect ecosystem consisting of:

- Faculty Connect Lounge
- Kids' Art Exhibition
- Faculty Podcast
- Mind Gym
- Connection Hours

The ecosystem is designed to encourage regular, low-pressure interactions that strengthen cross-department collaboration and faculty well-being.
